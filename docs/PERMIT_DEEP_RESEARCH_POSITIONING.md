# Permit Deep Research positioning

Status: launch decision  
Decision date: 2026-08-22  
Applies to: customer-facing PermitPulse surfaces

## Decision record

PermitPulse will lead with one transaction: **Permit Deep Research**, a manually reviewed, source-backed brief for California residential contractors and small builders evaluating an address before they bid, buy, or build.

The founding offer already supported by repository evidence is **three California addresses for $299 total**, with a target delivery of **48 business hours per address** after scope confirmation and receipt of required information. The website uses a request/review intake because the repository does not establish a working payment mechanism for this offer. Submission does not imply acceptance or payment.

The strongest existing proof is the **anonymized Los Angeles hillside property research reconstruction**. It demonstrates actual research judgment while protecting the property and customer. The canonical public PDF uses fictional records and is proof of format only.

This replaces competing customer-facing positions: the $249 stuck-permit file review, $149 Permit Review Plus links, $29 subscription, instant snapshot, free directional pointer, express tier, and Mission Control/dashboard calls to action. Internal case-workspace tooling remains an operational system, not the customer promise.

## Major objections and responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “Can’t I search the portal myself?” | Yes. PermitPulse is for decisions that require records from multiple routes, a dated timeline, visible conflicts and gaps, and prepared next questions—not a link to one portal. |
| “Does this prove the work is legal or buildable?” | No. The brief separates what sources document from inference and unknowns; agencies and licensed professionals make official and professional determinations. |
| “Will every record be included?” | No completeness promise. Some records are unavailable online, restricted, delayed, or require authorization or agency processing. Those limits remain explicit. |
| “Is this instant or automated?” | No. It is manually reviewed research with a stated delivery target after scope and required information are confirmed. |
| “Why three addresses?” | It fits contractors and small builders who screen more than one opportunity and preserves the legitimate founding offer already present in the repository. |
| “Do I pay when I submit?” | No. The intake is a scope request. A person reviews fit before acceptance and payment are arranged. |
| “Is PermitPulse a government partner?” | No. PermitPulse is independent and not affiliated with a permitting agency. |

## Safe wording boundaries

Use: Permit Deep Research; source-backed brief; manually reviewed; available public records; retrieval date; verified fact; reasonable inference; unknown; conflicts and gaps; questions to ask next; target delivery; request a scope review.

Do not claim or imply: guaranteed approval; completeness; code compliance; legal conclusion; ownership or access determination; entitlement certainty; “permit ready”; instant answer; autonomous learning; government affiliation; replacement of an architect, attorney, engineer, contractor, surveyor, title professional, or agency.

“Not found online” always means an evidence gap, never proof that a record does not exist.

## Brand architecture

- **ENM Ventures**: the operator building research-led local growth systems.
- **PermitPulse**: ENM Ventures’ permit and property-record research brand.
- **Permit Deep Research**: PermitPulse’s initial paid service and research method.
- **Permit Drops, Paper Trail Playbooks, Permit Nightmares**: PermitPulse’s three public content lanes.
- **SGV Turf**: a separate rebate-first local lead funnel. It must not appear as a PermitPulse service, share PermitPulse customer-facing positioning, or be mixed into PermitPulse intake and reporting.
