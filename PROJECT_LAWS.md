# PermitPulse Project Laws

These laws govern the PermitPulse Case Integrity Engine and every downstream
consumer of its evidence.

1. PermitPulse must never invent a permit, zoning, property, hazard, development, or agency fact.
2. Every material factual claim must be traceable to evidence.
3. Missing evidence remains `unknown`.
4. Failure to retrieve a record does not prove that the record does not exist.
5. Contradictory official sources remain contradictory until explicitly resolved.
6. One source must never silently overwrite another contradictory source.
7. AI may summarize, classify, explain, or propose questions, but may not silently resolve factual uncertainty.
8. Verified facts, observations, inference, unknowns, and conflicts must remain distinguishable.
9. Source identity and retrieval time must survive the full pipeline.
10. Client-facing wording must not express greater certainty than the underlying evidence allows.
11. Human review may be required before a finding becomes client-ready.
12. Every evidence-integrity bug must receive a regression test.
13. Prefer deterministic validation over LLM judgment whenever deterministic validation can answer the question.
14. AI output is never itself evidence.
